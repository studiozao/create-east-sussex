What is it

Support built around your creative business

Create East Sussex is a free business support programme that delivers tangible outputs for your business, connects you to mentors and peers, and provides the platform for you to collaborate on and share ideas for the future of the creative industries in the region.

You can take part in practical sessions called Connects, and explore more focused help where it is needed through mentoring. Either way, you become part of a growing network of creative businesses across East Sussex, connecting with each other and with mentors as well as with us, long after your Connect or mentoring ends.

Connects

Free · open to all · 2 hours · small-group sessions

Come together with other local founders to explore a real business challenge, share ideas and leave with useful next steps. Each Connect combines practical activity, useful input and time to meet people facing similar challenges.

Topics may include pricing, finding customers, marketing, funding, AI tools and growing with confidence.

Connects are the best place to start and can help us understand whether mentoring would be useful for your business.

See below for the upcoming Connects

Mentoring

Free · up to 6 hours · tailored support · 1-to-1

If mentoring is the right next step, we’ll work with you on the challenge that matters most to your business.

Support may include Studio Zao-led accountability and delivery coaching, broader business advice, or an introduction to an experienced creative peer depending on your needs.

You do not need to have all the answers before you apply. If you cannot attend a Connect, you can register your interest in mentoring directly and we will get in touch to understand your situation.

Can’t make it to a Connect? Email us and we’ll set you up with a business diagnostic instead.

Is Create East Sussex for you?

  • Actively trading creative business based in East Sussex
  • Working in design, digital, craft, fashion, film, gaming, music, publishing or a related creative field, including tech-enabled work like web or app design
  • Sole trader, freelancer, small business, social enterprise, community interest company (CIC), co-operative or charity (trading arm)

Not sure if you fit? Get in touch and we’ll help you work it out.

Frequently asked questions

What do I actually get out of it?

A free Connect session, access to the local network, and where appropriate up to six hours of tailored 1:1 mentoring.

Mentoring places are limited, so not everyone who registers is guaranteed one. Everyone who registers stays connected to Connects and the wider network either way.

Do I need to attend a Connect before mentoring?

Connects are the best place to start because they help us understand your business and what support would be most useful. If you cannot attend, you can still register your interest in mentoring directly.

Can’t make it to a Connect but still want mentoring? Email us and we’ll set you up with a business diagnostic instead.

How much time does it take?

Connects last two hours. Mentoring is up to six hours in total, arranged around your needs and availability.

Is it free?

Yes. Connects, mentoring and network participation are free.

What happens after I register?

We will contact you with details for the Connect you signed up to. If mentoring looks like the right next step, we will arrange a conversation to understand your priorities and discuss the available support.

Can everyone receive mentoring?

No. Mentoring places are limited and offered where there is a good fit, so not everyone who signs up will get one. Everyone who registers can still stay connected to Connects and the wider network.

Become a mentor

Could your experience help another creative business grow?

We’re looking for experienced creative business owners and professionals to volunteer as mentors with Create East Sussex. You’ll be matched with a local creative business and provide practical, one-to-one support to help them work through challenges, build confidence and move their business forward.

Why become a mentor?

  • Give something back by supporting another creative business.
  • Share your experience and lessons you’ve learnt along the way.
  • Make a difference to a local founder or business owner.
  • Build new connections across East Sussex’s creative community.
  • Keep it flexible: provide up to 6 hours of mentoring over 12 weeks.

Who are we looking for?

You’ll need:

  • At least 2 years’ creative business experience.
  • A willingness to listen, encourage and share your experience.
  • Availability to provide up to 6 hours of voluntary mentoring over 12 weeks.

Interested? Sign up to become a Create East Sussex Volunteer Mentor.
