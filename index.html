How it works

Your route to one-to-one mentoring

Free 1:1 mentoring is how you get here. Getting started is simple. Come to a Connect, free and open to anyone, meet people, and see whether going deeper is right for you. No pressure either way.

1

Get invited to mentoring

Once you’ve been to a Connect, or simply told us you’re interested, you’re invited to start mentoring.

2

Go through the diagnostic

A short survey, then a 30-minute call to talk through your situation and agree the focus for your mentoring Sprint.

3

Start your Sprint

You’re matched with a mentor and begin your 6-hour Sprint, at whatever pace suits your practice: a few short weekly sessions, or a couple of longer deep-dives.

4

Stay part of the network

Once your Sprint ends, you’re an alum: you’ll get a short summary of next steps, keep your invite to future Connects, and, if it’s a fit, come back later as a mentor yourself.

Places on the mentoring Sprint are limited and matched to fit, so we can’t guarantee mentoring for everyone who applies. Everyone’s welcome at the Connects and the wider network regardless.

And through all of it, you’re part of something bigger: a community of creative people across East Sussex, at the Connects, in the mentoring, and beyond. You’re not doing this on your own.
