What you get

Three ways in, all free

Free 1:1 mentoring is the heart of it, and you can go straight there. Practical sessions and a network of people who get it sit alongside, for whenever you want them. Connects are free, 2-hour workshops. Open to anyone, no booking history needed.

Connects

Open to everyone · 2 hours · free

Free, 2-hour practical sessions, and the easiest way in. Come away with something tangible on pricing, audience building, hiring, AI tools and accessing grants.

Mentoring

Rolling sign-up · up to 6 hours, one-to-one

The heart of the programme. Get matched with a mentor who has built a creative business themselves, and work on whatever your business actually needs, including pricing, finding customers, marketing, AI, productivity, confidence and business growth. Register and we’ll take it from there.

A growing network

Free · ongoing

Open to any actively trading creative business in East Sussex. Meet other creatives and swap ideas and referrals, a network that carries on long after your mentoring ends.

Check you’re eligible

  • Actively trading creative business based in East Sussex
  • Working in design, digital, craft, fashion, film, gaming, music, publishing or a related creative field, including tech-enabled work like web or app design (general IT support doesn’t count)
  • Sole trader, freelancer, small business, social enterprise, co-operative or charity (trading arm)

Not sure if you fit? Get in touch and we’ll help you work it out.

Your business, your journey

You join because there’s something specific you’re trying to work out: pricing, finding customers, getting your marketing straight, building the confidence to grow. You get matched with a mentor and work through it over a 6-hour Sprint, at whatever pace suits your practice.

Along the way, you’re part of something bigger too: a community of creative people across East Sussex, in the mentoring, at the Connects, and beyond. The community is what makes the support last. Once your Sprint ends, you keep your invite to future Connects, and you’re welcome to come back later as a mentor yourself.

Mentoring places are limited and matched to fit, so we can’t promise one to everyone who applies. The Connects and the wider network stay open to you either way.

Frequently asked questions

What do I actually get out of it?

Free 1:1 mentoring tailored to where your business is right now, practical Connects sessions on things like pricing, audience building, hiring, AI tools and grants, and an ongoing network of other creative businesses across East Sussex.

How much time does it take?

Connects are 2 hours each. Mentoring is up to 6 hours total, split however suits you, a few short weekly sessions or a couple of longer deep-dives.

Is there any cost?

No. Everything on this programme, mentoring, Connects and the network, is free.

What happens after I register?

You register in a couple of minutes. Then a short survey and a 30-minute call to talk through your situation and agree the focus for your mentoring Sprint. You’re matched with a mentor and begin your Sprint. Once it ends, you’re an alum: you’ll get a short summary of next steps, keep your invite to future Connects, and, if it’s a fit, come back later as a mentor yourself.

Do I have to come to a Connects before I get mentoring?

No. Meeting us at a Connect first is welcome, but it’s not a step you have to take.
