Join the community. Get mentoring because of it.

You don’t need to be sold a workshop. You need people who get it, and support that comes from being part of something: that’s the Collective, and everything else follows from being in it.

The Collective

Free to join · ongoing

Free to join, open to any actively trading creative business in East Sussex. Meet other creatives, build relationships that last, and get matched with one-to-one mentoring once you’re part of it. This is the network that carries on long after the programme ends.

Hacks

Open to everyone · 2 hours · free

Free, 2-hour working sessions, and the easiest way in. Real challenges, tangible takeaways, and a room full of peers rather than a lecture, covering pricing, audience building, hiring, AI tools and accessing grants, across Lewes, Hastings and Eastbourne.

Mentoring Sprints

By invitation · 6 hours, one-to-one

Six hours of one-to-one time with a mentor who has built a creative business themselves. A short diagnostic call comes first to find your focus, then structured sessions built entirely around what you need.

Your route to one-to-one mentoring

Being part of the Collective is how you get here: real mentoring, matched to your actual needs. Getting started is simple. Come to a Hack, free and open to anyone, meet people, and see whether going deeper is right for you. No pressure either way.

1

Get invited to mentoring

Once you’ve been to a Hack, or simply told us you’re interested, you’re invited to start mentoring.

2

Go through the diagnostic

A short survey, then a 30-minute call to talk through your situation and agree the focus for your mentoring Sprint.

3

Start your Sprint

You’re matched with a mentor and begin your 6-hour Sprint, at whatever pace suits your practice: a few short weekly sessions, or a couple of longer deep-dives.

4

Stay part of the Collective

Once your Sprint ends, you’re an alum: you’ll get a short summary of next steps, keep your invite to future Hacks, and, if it’s a fit, come back later as a mentor yourself.

Places on the mentoring Sprint are limited and matched to fit, so we can’t guarantee mentoring for everyone who applies. Everyone’s welcome at the Hacks and the wider Collective regardless.

And through all of it, you’re part of something bigger: a community of creative people across East Sussex, at the Hacks, in the mentoring, and beyond. You’re not doing this on your own.

Is this for you?

  • Actively trading creative business based in East Sussex
  • Working in design, digital, craft, fashion, film, gaming, music, publishing or a related creative field, including tech-enabled work like web or app design (general IT support doesn’t count)
  • Sole trader, freelancer, small business, social enterprise, co-operative or charity (trading arm)

Not sure if you fit? Come along to a Hack or get in touch, and we’ll help you work it out.
