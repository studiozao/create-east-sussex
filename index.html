About our team

Create East Sussex is delivered by Studio Zao, an innovation and growth consultancy, in partnership with East Sussex County Council.

Who we are

We’re a nimble team focused on making good ideas happen.

We’ve worked with corporations, the public sector and SMEs, always keeping things grounded in real-world experience, not theory.

Our approach is people-first: understanding customers and users is at the heart of finding real solutions.

We believe successful growth comes from entrepreneurial mindsets: testing, learning, and staying solution-oriented.

What we do

We help businesses turn ideas into investable, scalable opportunities through programmes, coaching and hands-on support.

From accelerators in Devon and Croydon to projects with the NHS, TfL and Sony, we support teams to test, prove and scale what works.

We also equip the public sector and large corporations with practical tools and entrepreneurial methods so they can keep creating new opportunities long after we’ve gone.

Who we’ve helped

We’ve supported hundreds of businesses to secure funding, launch products and grow with confidence.

We’ve worked with everyone from manufacturers like Hymid to night-time venues, creatives, tech start-ups and social enterprises, helping with raising investment, positioning new offers and testing ideas with customers.

Backed by our innovation and growth expertise, we’ve delivered with partners including Innovate UK, UKSPF-funded councils, UKRI and Sport England.

Your route to one-to-one mentoring

Getting started is simple, and it is the same short route whether you have been to a Connect or you are coming to us cold. Tell us you are interested and we will pick it up from there.

1

Tell us you’re interested

Register in a couple of minutes. Meeting us at a Connect first is welcome, but it is not a step you have to take.

2

Go through the diagnostic

A short survey, then a 30-minute call to talk through your situation and agree the focus for your mentoring Sprint.

3

Start your Sprint

You’re matched with a mentor and begin your 6-hour Sprint, at whatever pace suits your practice: a few short weekly sessions, or a couple of longer deep-dives.

4

Stay part of the network

Once your Sprint ends, you’re an alum: you’ll get a short summary of next steps, keep your invite to future Connects, and, if it’s a fit, come back later as a mentor yourself.

Mentoring places are limited and matched to fit, so we can’t promise one to everyone who applies. The Connects and the wider network stay open to you either way.

And through all of it, you’re part of something bigger: a community of creative people across East Sussex, in the mentoring, at the Connects, and beyond. You’re not doing this on your own.
